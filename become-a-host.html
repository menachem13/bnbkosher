Frequently Asked Questions

Can I list a property without a kosher kitchen?

Yes. You can note your kitchen setup so guests know exactly what to expect.

Do I need to live near my vacation property?

No. Many hosts manage their listings remotely with the help of local contacts or property managers.

Can I mention nearby shuls and eruv information?

Yes. Sharing nearby shuls, eruv boundaries, and walking distances helps guests plan their stay.

How much does it cost to become a host?

Creating a listing is free. You only pay a small service fee once you start receiving bookings.
